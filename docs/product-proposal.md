# Product Proposal: Midnight Private Payroll & Splits

## 1. Problem Statement

In conventional corporate payroll systems, employee compensation data is vulnerable to pervasive privacy exposure. Bank transfers, accounting ledgers, human resource databases, and public blockchain transactions reveal exact salary figures, payment frequencies, and organizational hierarchy.

On transparent blockchains, financial auditing provides high integrity, but at the unacceptable cost of absolute financial exposure: any observer can see exact employee pay rates, bonus payments, and corporate financial health.

Businesses and employees need a system where:
1. **Compliance can be proven:** Proving that compensation adheres to budgetary ceilings and company policies without audits requiring full salary disclosure.
2. **Double-spending / duplicate splits are prevented:** Ensuring that an employee's salary slice is registered exactly once per cycle.
3. **Data remains strictly confidential:** Ensuring that competitors, colleagues, and public observers cannot observe individual salaries.

---

## 2. Proposed Solution

**Midnight Private Payroll** solves this dilemma using Midnight's zero-knowledge Compact smart contracts. By evaluating business rules off-chain within client-side zk-SNARK circuits and committing only 256-bit cryptographic hashes on-chain:
- Employees and employers prove compliance with policy thresholds ($0 < \text{salary} \le \text{ceiling}$) mathematically.
- The Midnight blockchain records an anonymized, collision-resistant commitment hash in a persistent on-chain set.
- Duplicate commitments are cryptographically rejected, preventing double-allocations.
- Public observers verify the validity of payroll allocations and cycle counters, while raw financial compensation figures remain strictly confidential to the participant.

| User Role | Responsibilities & Benefits |

| **Employers & Executives** | Enforce corporate compensation ceilings and budget allocations across departments without exposing sensitive executive and departmental compensation sheets. |
| **Payroll Administrators** | Register verifiable payroll split commitments, advance distribution cycles, and prove regulatory compliance without maintaining unencrypted salary databases. |

## 4. Privacy Model

### Observer Can Directly See (Public On-Chain)
- **Deployed Contract Address:** The unique Midnight smart contract identity.
- **Circuit Verifier Keys:** Cryptographic keys proving that the execution followed the Compact contract rules.
- **Public Split Counter (`split_count`):** Total count of valid private payroll allocations committed.
- **Public Verification Counter (`verification_count`):** Total count of zero-knowledge compliance proofs verified.
- **Payroll Cycle (`payroll_cycle`):** Current accounting period index (`#0`, `#1`, `#2`, etc.).
- **Public Policy Thresholds:** The ceiling argument passed to the circuit (e.g. `maxAllowedSalary = 10000`).
- **Cryptographic Commitment Hashes:** 32-byte hashes in `split_commitments` set.

### Observer Cannot Directly See (Confidential Off-Chain)
- **Raw Employee Salary Figures:** The numeric compensation amount.
- **Private Witness Inputs:** Values ingested through `get_salary_amount()` during zk-SNARK synthesis.
- **Blinding Salts (Nonces):** Random 32-byte nonces ingested through `get_split_nonce()` ensuring commitment indistinguishability.
- **Prover Wire Assignments:** Intermediate polynomial evaluations generated by the proof server.
- **Client Private State:** Local LevelDB encrypted state storage on the user's workstation.

## 5. Current Implementation Status

The current repository contains a fully working, production-grade implementation of the core privacy workflow:
- **Compact Contract (`contract/contracts/payroll.compact`):** Contains `record_private_split`, `verify_salary`, and `advance_payroll_cycle` circuits with state constraints and set-based duplicate prevention.
- **Zero-Knowledge Key Infrastructure:** Prover keys, verifier keys, and ZKIR binaries compiled and served via Next.js public routes.
- **MidnightJS Provider Integration:** Robust integration connecting Midnight Lace wallet, LevelDB encrypted private storage, indexer GraphQL queries, and local proof servers.
- **Interactive UI Dashboard (`components/private-payroll-dashboard.tsx`):** Complete Next.js application supporting wallet connection, contract deployment, contract join, private split recording, and salary verification.
- **Test Coverage:** 77 passing automated tests covering all circuits, witness injection, network adapters, and edge-case assertion failures.
- **Public Network Deployment:** Deployed and verified on the official Midnight Preview Testnet.

---

## 6. Current Limitation

> **Important Architecture Limitation:**  
> The current version of Midnight Private Payroll records and verifies zero-knowledge payroll split commitments and enforces policy compliance on-chain. It does **not** automatically execute native token fund transfers to employee addresses.

---

## 7. Future Extensions (Roadmap)

Planned future evolutions following the core Level 3 submission:
1. **Automated Shielded Settlement:** Integrating Midnight shielded token transfer primitives (Zswap outputs) directly within the `record_private_split` circuit to atomically pay the employee while recording the split commitment.
2. **Multi-Recipient Batched Payroll:** Allowing administrators to submit bulk batches of encrypted employee splits in a single transaction with aggregated zk-SNARK proofs.
3. **Selective Disclosure & Attestation:** Providing verifiable cryptographic viewing keys allowing an employee to selectively reveal their compensation history to specific third parties (e.g., mortgage lenders or tax authorities) without public leakage.
